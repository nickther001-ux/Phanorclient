Subject: Website Design & Infrastructure 



Brief: Phanor Distribution Inc. & Book Honors



**Project Goal:** To build a high-performance, "Industrial-Luxury" digital flagship that serves as a strategic distribution hub for B2B (Restaurants/Markets) and B2C (Households) clients. This site must integrate with our automation stack (Vocable/SocialBee) and establish our identity as a vertically integrated meat and seafood empire.

### 1. VISUAL IDENTITY & BRAND AESTHETIC

 * **The Vibe:** "The Architect meets the Industrialist." Clean, authoritative, and premium.

 * **Palette:** * **Primary:** Onyx Black (#1A1A1A)

   * **Accent:** Sovereign Gold (#C5A059) – for the Crown & Bull logo and CTAs.

   * **Text:** Architect White (#F5F5F5).

 * **Typography:** Bold headlines (*Montserrat* or *Bebas Neue*) with clean, professional body text (*Roboto*).

 * **Imagery:** High-contrast, professional photography of premium cuts (marbling), industrial vacuum-sealed packaging, and "Behind the Scenes" of our logistics and farm expansion.

### 2. CORE ARCHITECTURE & FUNCTIONALITY

 * **Client Portal (The Gatekeeper):** * Secure Login/Account creation.

   * **Dual-Interface:** System must detect and display different pricing for B2B (Wholesale/Bulk cases) vs. B2C (Retail Bundles).

   * **One-Click Reorder:** Feature for repeat B2B clients to re-submit their weekly standing orders.

 * **Payment & Logistics:** * Full integration with Stripe/Square.

   * **Zone-Based Shipping:** Automated logic that calculates delivery dates/eligibility based on the user's postal code (Montreal Zones 1-5).

 * **The Smart Inventory Machine:** Real-time stock counts that trigger "Low Stock" alerts to drive urgency.

### 3. THE "PHANOR AI" ASSISTANT (Key Feature)

We require a custom AI widget integrated into the shop:

 * **Consumption Calculator:** Users input guest count and meat type; the AI calculates the exact raw weight needed (accounting for bone/cook loss).

 * **Bundle Generator:** AI suggests a custom "Random Box" based on a user’s specific budget (e.g., "$200 for the month").

### 4. STRATEGIC GROWTH FEATURES (Suggestion 2)

 * **The Sovereign Circle Referral Loop:** A backend system that generates unique referral codes for every account.

   * **B2B:** Referrers earn "Phanor Credits" applied to future invoices.

   * **B2C:** Tiered status (Bronze to Gold) that unlocks permanent discounts and exclusive "Farm Access" cuts.

 * **SMS "Drop" Integration:** A sign-up field for instant text alerts on rare meat arrivals.

### 5. SITE MAP

 1. **Home:** The "Hero" video of the Crown & Bull seal and the Farm-to-Table mission.

 2. **The Shop:** Divided into "Sélect Bundles" (B2C) and "Wholesale Cases" (B2B).

 3. **Book Honors:** A luxury landing page for high-end catering inquiries.

 4. **The Logistics:** A transparency page explaining the "Phanor Standard" (Packaging & Quality Control).

 5. **About:** The story of Widler Junior Phanor—8 years of evolution from a single butcher to a multi-channel empire.
